      01
      
Who you're hiring

    

    

      

        
Most "automation" is a spreadsheet with extra steps.
        I build the thing that removes the step.

        
I'm a self-taught developer who fell into bot making because it's the
        fastest way to turn a real annoyance into a real fix. Give me a repetitive
        task and a chat window, and I'll give you a bot that handles it.

        
I care about the unglamorous parts: retries, rate limits, error messages a
        human can actually understand, logs you can read at 2am. A bot that works on
        a good day isn't finished.

        

          Fast to ship
          Documented
          Self-hosted friendly
          No lock-in

      04
      
How we work

    

    

      

        01
        
You describe the annoyance

        
A voice note, a paragraph, a mess of screenshots. I don't need a spec.

      

      

        02
        
I scope it honestly

        
What's automatable, what isn't, what it'll cost, roughly how long. In writing, before any code.

      

      

        03
        
You get a working bot fast

        
A usable v1 in your own chat within days — not a slide deck about a bot.

      

      

        04
        
We harden it

        
Retries, permissions, backups, docs. You own the code and the hosting either way.
